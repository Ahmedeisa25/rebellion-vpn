1. Scope

This policy explains how the Rebellion VPN browser extension for Google Chrome and this website handle information. Rebellion VPN is an independent browser extension project; in this policy, “we” and “us” refer to its developer.

Rebellion VPN is a location switcher for a compatible VPN connection that you already use. It is not a VPN service provider and does not operate the VPN tunnel.

2. Summary

At a glance

No account. No analytics or telemetry. No advertising. No sale of data. Your settings stay in Chrome's local extension storage. The extension contacts only the endpoints needed for its features — the server list, latency checks, and an exit-IP check — which belong to the VPN service you are using.

3. Information stored on your device

The extension uses chrome.storage.local. This data stays on your computer inside your Chrome profile and is not synced to us. It includes:

  • Settings — auto-connect on startup, WebRTC leak protection, and your selected server.
  • Favorites — locations or servers you star.
  • Recent locations — servers you connected to recently.
  • Cached server list — server names, locations, and the time it was last updated, so the popup opens quickly.
  • Latency results — approximate response times from speed tests, cached for about 15 minutes.
  • Connection state — whether the extension believes it is connected, and the exit IP and location returned by the connection check while the popup is open.
  • Original proxy and WebRTC settings — saved only so they can be restored when you disconnect or remove the extension.

You can clear favorites and recent locations in Settings. Removing the extension deletes all of its local data.

4. Network requests

To work, the extension must talk to the VPN service that provides your connection. It does not send your data to a Rebellion VPN server — we do not operate one.

Request Why When
Server list To show locations and available servers. At startup, and when you choose Refresh Server List.
Latency checks To estimate which servers respond fastest. When you test servers or use Fastest server.
Exit IP check To display your exit IP and location after connecting. When you connect or refresh the connection state.

These requests go directly to endpoints operated by your VPN service. Like any web request, they necessarily reveal your IP address and standard technical details (such as your user agent) to that service. That service handles the request under its own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy, and we do not log these requests.

The exit IP shown in the popup is displayed locally. It is not transmitted to us or stored anywhere other than your browser's local extension storage.

Important

Your VPN service can see your traffic while connected, exactly as it would without this extension. Choose a VPN service you trust.

5. What we don't collect

  • No browsing history, page contents, form data, or keystrokes.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising identifiers.
  • No account, email address, or payment information.
  • No precise location from your device.
  • No data sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

The extension does not inject ads, change search results, or track you across sites.

6. Permissions and why they are needed

  • proxy — to apply the location you choose to Chrome's connection settings. This is the core feature.
  • storage — to keep your settings, favorites, and recent locations locally.
  • privacy — to enable or disable WebRTC leak protection using Chrome's privacy settings.
  • Host access to your VPN service's endpoints — limited to the server list, latency checks, and exit-IP check described above.

7. Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. Because we do not operate a collection server, there is no database of user activity for us to share.

We may disclose information only if we are legally required to and actually hold it — which, by design, is effectively nothing beyond what is already on your device.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear it, remove the extension, or delete your Chrome profile. To delete everything:

  1. Open chrome://extensions.
  2. Find Rebellion VPN and choose Remove.

9. This website

This site is a static website. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. It loads no third-party fonts or CDN resources.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) to deliver the site, under GitHub's privacy statement. Links to external sites, including GitHub, are governed by those sites' policies.

10. Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

11. Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised. Material changes will also be noted in the extension's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or a privacy concern? Open an issue on GitHub. Please do not include credentials, your exact IP address, or other personal information.
