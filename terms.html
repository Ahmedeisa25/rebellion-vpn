2. What the extension is

Rebellion VPN is a browser extension that switches Chrome's connection location for a compatible VPN connection that you already have. It is not a VPN service provider and does not create, operate, or secure the VPN tunnel.

In plain terms

You bring the VPN connection. Rebellion VPN changes where your browser connects through it.
